**May 2026 — Present**

An Android conversation intelligence platform that turns recordings into speaker-attributed transcripts, structured summaries, participant viewpoints, and timelines. Built across the mobile client, asynchronous audio pipeline, and cloud backend.

## Technical work

- Developed the Android client and cloud backend, including REST APIs, task-state management, and asynchronous audio processing.
- Combined Paraformer speech recognition, CAM++ speaker matching, and a Qwen-powered agent to generate structured reports.
- Deployed with Docker, Nginx, and HTTPS on Alibaba Cloud.
- Tested with 5 users across more than 50 recordings, including sessions up to 90 minutes, with end-to-end processing below 5 minutes.


**Technologies / topics:** Android, ASR, Cloud
