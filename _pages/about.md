I’m **Jianyu Long**, and I go by **Westbrook**. I study **Data Science and Computer Science at the University of Wisconsin–Madison**. Previously, I studied Data Science and Big Data Technology at the Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech).

My work connects AI models with the systems around them: how agents remember across sessions, how speech becomes useful information, and how research implementations become runnable tools. I enjoy building across the stack, from data pipelines and model training to desktop and mobile applications.

## Research interests

- **LLM agents and persistent memory:** evidence-governed memory updates, hybrid retrieval, tool use, and evaluation-feedback workflows.
- **Speech and conversation intelligence:** real-time voice input, speaker-attributed transcription, and structured conversation understanding.
- **Applied machine learning:** Transformer architectures, recommendation systems, mathematical reasoning, and multimodal time-series modeling.

## Selected work

- [AI Pet](/portfolio/ai-pet/): a desktop companion agent with persistent memory, hybrid retrieval, and permission-aware tool use.
- [Chastream](/portfolio/chastream/): an Android platform for speaker-attributed transcripts and structured conversation reports.
- [Flow Voice](/portfolio/flow-voice/): open-source voice input that streams smartphone speech recognition to a Windows cursor.
- [Personalized Training Planning Agent](/portfolio/training-planning-agent/): a retrieval-augmented planning workflow with an evaluation-feedback loop.

See all [projects](/portfolio/) and [research implementations](/research/).

## Background

In summer 2025, I interned at **Shenzhen Streamap Technology Co., Ltd.**, where I developed a road-roughness estimation pipeline using vehicle-mounted IMU data and a Transformer–LSTM model. The work involved more than **7 million multimodal data points** and received an **Outstanding Intern Award**.

I also earned a **Kaggle Silver Medal** in the 2024 AI Mathematical Olympiad competition, ranking **92nd out of 2,212 teams**.
